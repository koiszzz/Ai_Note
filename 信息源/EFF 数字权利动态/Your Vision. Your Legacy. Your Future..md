<div class="field field--name-body field--type-text-with-summary field--label-hidden"><div class="field__items"><div class="field__item even"><p>This month, <a href="https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/07/building-our-future-together">we celebrate 36 years of EFF</a> and a mission that is bigger than any one of us. Thanks to EFF, communities around the world are demanding that technology protects their freedom, advances justice, and opens doors to opportunity. That's not a small thing—it's a life's work worth continuing.</p>

<p>If you are committed to staying on the cutting edge of digital rights issues, I'd like to invite you to consider taking that commitment one step further by joining <a href="https://www.eff.org/givingsociety/lighthouse">EFF’s Lighthouse Society</a>, our way to acknowledge and thank the community of supporters who are including EFF in their legacy plans.</p>

<p class="take-action"><a href="https://eff.org/lighthouse">Learn About the Lighthouse Society</a></p>

<p>By including EFF in your will or estate plans, you can ensure that EFF’s work and values don't just live beyond you; they thrive because of you. A legacy gift is one of the most powerful ways to say: <em>This matters, and I want it to matter long after I'm gone.</em></p>

<p>Your gift will fuel our mission for generations by protecting freedom, advancing justice, and driving innovation for communities who need it most. There is still so much more to do, so much more to fight for. With your foresight, it can go so much further.</p>

<p>Planned giving is also more flexible than you might realize. A bequest in your will, a simple beneficiary designation, or another estate planning option can all make a profound difference, often without affecting your finances today.</p>

<p>Get in touch and learn more about what's possible with the Lighthouse Society. Reach out to Jocelyn Wicker at <a href="mailto:majorgifts@eff.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">majorgifts@eff.org</a> or <a href="https://eff.org/lighthouse">fill out our online form</a> to share your intention to give. Thank you for considering a legacy that will carry this work forward for years to come.</p>
