# Discretization and Statistical Consistency of Functional Flow Matching

arXiv:2608.04531v1 Announce Type: new

Abstract: Functional flow matching is posed on distributions of functions but implemented from finitely many coefficients or point values. Under scattered or adaptive refinement, the resulting conditioning sigma-algebras need not be nested, so martingale convergence does not justify the sensor limit. We prove strong $L^2$ convergence of finite conditional velocity targets for every strongly consistent sequence of finite-rank reconstructions, with quantitative bounds for orthogonal projections and a point-sensor extension through a regularity space. For learned flows, coupling directly to a population superposition path yields an end-to-end Wasserstein bound without assuming uniqueness of the population finite-dimensional ODE. We verify sensor-independent constants for a normalized quadrature neural operator, including globally Lipschitz activations through an explicit magnitude recurrence. A noncommuting trace-class Gaussian example gives boundary multiplier $0$ under projected restriction and $0.72$ under exact conditioning. A spatial regularity--cubature certificate closes the operator-realization term, a Bernstein argument gives a $\widetilde{O}(n^{-1})$ excess-risk term for fixed model dimension and envelopes, and an exactly realizable clipped Gaussian scaling specialization yields an explicit end-to-end rate.
